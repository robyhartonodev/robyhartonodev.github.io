About

I'm a backend-focused developer with 3+ years of professional experience building Java/Spring Boot services and cloud solutions on AWS and Azure for enterprise clients, including Bosch and a major German insurance company.

My M.Sc. in Medical Informatics and four years digitalizing clinical processes at a hospital taught me to build for environments where reliability and data protection aren't optional. I also work across the stack with Angular, so I design APIs with the people who use them in mind.
